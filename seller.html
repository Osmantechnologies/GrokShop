How selling works

Shipping within a working week earns the X fast badge. Grok only shows what you say here.

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live. No passwords, keys or bank details are asked for here.

Orders with your products. Ship to the address shown, then mark each item shipped; the buyer sees it (and the tracking number) in Your orders.

Already on Shopify? Import your catalogue instead of listing by hand. Add Shopify's free Headless channel, publish your products to it, and copy its public Storefront access token.

Never paste the private Admin API token (it starts with shpat_) or your Shopify login. Grokazon refuses them.
