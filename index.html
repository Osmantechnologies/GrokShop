        FAQ

        How is this different from Amazon?

        Grokazon is a store like Amazon, with one cart and one checkout, but Grok is built in: tell it what you need and it shops every seller here for you, shows why it picked what it picked, and handles missions and returns. And bots are welcome: Amazon is blocking them.

        Who are the merchants?

        Independent stores that join with their own API (Shopify today), sellers who list directly on Grokazon, and Grokazon's own US-warehouse shelf. Grokazon never scrapes a store.

        Does Grok see my card?

        No. Grok builds the cart; you pay on the merchant's own checkout. Grokazon has no payment form anywhere.

        What does X get me?

        Fast delivery is included with an X subscription. Sign in with X and eligible items show the X fast badge.

        Why is it called Grokazon?

        Because of a thread on X on Oct 15, 2026. Elon Musk asked whether to start a competitor to Amazon after Amazon began blocking Grok Bots, and @thecsguy replied "grokazon!", adding that fast delivery should be included in your X subscription. This is that idea, as a concept.

        What is a mission?

        A shopping job Grok keeps running: a budget, a deadline and what to watch for. It reports back when it finds the right thing or the right price.
