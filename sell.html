How a store joins

With permission, through your store's own API. Grokazon never scrapes a store.

01

Ask to join

Send your store address below. Requests are read by hand while Grokazon is a concept.

02

Connect with permission

Once approved, you add Shopify's free Headless channel and share its public, read-only Storefront token. You stay in control and can disconnect any time.

03

Shoppers check out with you

Grok builds the cart and hands the shopper to your checkout. You handle payment, shipping and returns as you do today.

A Shopify store

02

The Headless channel

Free from Shopify. Add it, then publish the products you want Grok to see to that channel.

03

Your public Storefront token

Headless shows it on its Storefront API page. It is read-only and made to be public. Never send the private token or your admin login.

04

Your delivery promise

How fast you ship (for example "1-2 business days") and your return window. Shopify does not publish these, and Grok only shows what you tell it.

Merchant FAQ

What does it cost?

Nothing while Grokazon is a concept. No fees have been set.

Which stores can join?

Shopify stores first, then other stores whose platform lets them share their catalogue through an API with permission.

Do I change my checkout?

No. Shoppers check out on your store exactly as they do today. Grokazon has no payment form.

How does Grok decide to show my product?

On fit with the shopper's request, budget and deadline, then price, delivery time, rating, returns and warranty. The reasoning is shown to the shopper next to every pick.

Can I leave?

Yes. Disconnect from your Shopify admin and your products stop appearing in Grok's answers.
