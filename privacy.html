Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026. Plain words, no tricks. Questions: @AdeliaCmartins on X.

What Grokshop keeps

  • Your X account, if you sign in with X. Through X's own login (read-only), Grokshop reads your name, @handle, profile picture and which X plan you have, once, to show your account and apply fast delivery. It does not post, read your posts or messages, or see your password, and it does not keep X's access token. A random Grokshop session id is kept for 30 days so you stay signed in.
  • A random browser id. Made in your browser so your cart, missions, orders and listings stay yours without an account. It says nothing about who you are.
  • Your delivery place. The city, country and postcode you choose (or this device's own guess from its time zone). Kept in your browser, and with each order you place.
  • Orders. What you bought, from which seller, the price, shipping, delivery place and status, for up to 400 days. Sellers see their own items in your order and where to ship them. Nothing else about you.
  • Missions, reviews and listings you create, until you remove them or they expire.
  • Searches you send to Grok. The words you type go to xAI's Grok (and, if Grok is down, to Anthropic's Claude) together with the matching products, to choose and explain picks. Answers are cached for 12 hours so the same question is not sent twice.
  • Counts, not people. The server counts page views, searches, orders, sign-ins, listings and reviews per day. Your internet address is used only to stop abuse (a daily limit per address) and is not stored with any of the above.

Payments

You type your shipping address on Grokshop's checkout page; it is saved with the order and passed to the payment provider (Stripe) so you don't type it twice. Card details are typed only on Stripe's own page, never on Grokshop, and Grokshop never stores a card. While Grokshop is in preview, payments run in Stripe's test mode: real cards are refused, nothing is charged and nothing ships. Preview orders, with their address, are deleted after 30 days. Your browser also remembers the last address you used, on this device only.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare (runs Grokshop's server and stores the records above), Netlify (serves the website).
  • X (sign-in), xAI and Anthropic (Grok's answers), Stripe (payments, once on).
  • Shopify stores and a public product catalogue (DummyJSON) that supply products and photos.

Grokshop does not sell your data and does not use advertising trackers or cookies. Your browser keeps a few settings (cart, delivery place, missions) in its own storage; clearing site data removes them.

Your choices

  • Sign out of X from the account menu at any time; the session is deleted.
  • Ask for your orders, reviews or listings to be deleted: message @AdeliaCmartins.
