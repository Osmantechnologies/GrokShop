What Grokazon is

Grokazon is a store where independent sellers list products and Grok, an AI shopping assistant, helps you find and compare them. Grok explains its picks, but it can be wrong: check the product page, the seller and the price before you buy. Grokazon is new; parts of it, such as payments, may still run in test mode, and test orders are always marked as such.

Buying

  • Each product is sold by the seller named on its page. Prices, stock, delivery times and return windows come from the seller.
  • Shipping follows the rule shown in the cart: free on $35 or more from one seller, free X fast delivery for items that ship within a working week when you are signed in with a paid X plan, otherwise $4.99 per seller. Taxes, where they apply, are shown on the payment page.
  • "X fast delivery" is Grokazon's own delivery benefit for X subscribers; it is offered by Grokazon, not by X.
  • Returns follow each seller's return window, shown on the product page. Grokazon's returns bot starts the return within that window.

Selling

  • Sellers must own or be allowed to sell what they list, describe it truthfully, use real photos, ship within the time they state, and honour their return window.
  • Every listing and every connected store is reviewed before it goes live, and can be removed if it breaks these terms or the law.
  • Shopify stores connect with their public, read-only Storefront token. Never share a private token or a login with Grokazon.
  • Sellers see the buyer's name and delivery address only for paid orders, and only to ship them.

Reviews

Only people who bought an item on Grokazon can review it, and each review is marked Verified purchase. Reviews must be honest and about the product; ones that are abusive or fake can be removed.
